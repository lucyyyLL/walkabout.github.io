About

Every building, navigable for everyone.

Finding the building isn't the same as reaching the room. A missing ramp, an unmarked entrance or a broken lift can end a trip, and the people most likely to meet them are the ones today's maps forget.

Why we're here

The shortest route isn't a usable one for millions of people

Every one of them has somewhere to be: a class, an appointment, a gate. We want the way there to be as clear for them as it is for anyone.

  • 1 in 4U.S. adults has a disabilityCDC, 2022 data: 28.7% of adults
  • 61 millionAmericans are 65 or older, and the number is growingU.S. Census Bureau, 2024
  • 59%of the people we surveyed had to stop and ask a stranger the wayWalkAbout questionnaire, 2026

Who we are

Architects and urbanists at MIT and Harvard

We read floor plans and sections every day, so we started where those drawings could help most: the handoffs between street, building and floor that no map shows.
